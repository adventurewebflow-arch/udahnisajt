Base camp

Your group wakes up already inside the park.

No two-hour transfer to the trailhead every morning. Our base sits at Tjentište, in the heart of Sutjeska — steps from the largest open-air pool in the Balkans, fed straight from the Sutjeska river.

  • 90 beds — cabins and ensuite rooms, plus a wild-camp option on the lake
  • Half board, shepherd breakfasts, welcome dinner
  • Several guides and vans — two coaches handled at once
  • Mixed-ability groups run the same day without splitting up
